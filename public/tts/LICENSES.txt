Third-party files served for the Listen player's neural voice (public/tts/).

1. ONNX Runtime Web 1.27.0, vendor/ort/ (ort.webgpu.min.js, ort-wasm-simd-threaded.asyncify.mjs,
   ort-wasm-simd-threaded.asyncify.wasm). Licence: MIT (vendor/ort/LICENSE-onnxruntime.txt). Copyright Microsoft.
   Byte-identical to npm onnxruntime-web@1.27.0; sha384 matches the pins in snippets/ort-web-*/_loader.js.
   Not committed yet (owner decides vendor vs CDN+SRI); see .gitignore.

2. Kokoro-82M model weights, voices and tokenizer: fetched from Hugging Face after the visitor consents
   (onnx-community/Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX, revision 1939ad2a8e416c0acfeecc08a694d14ef25f2231), not stored here.
   Licence: Apache-2.0. Speech by Kokoro-82M (hexgrad, Apache-2.0), ONNX export by onnx-community.
   Training data includes Koniwa (CC BY 3.0) and SIWIS (CC BY 4.0).

3. Pronunciation lexicon, lexicon-en-us.txt (4.6 MB, not committed yet, see .gitignore): derived from misaki's us_gold and us_silver dictionaries
   (hexgrad/misaki, Apache-2.0). The package does not document the provenance of its dictionary entries.

4. Kokoro G2P and normaliser in kokoro_glue.js and kokoro_bg.wasm (Brev, Rust/WASM): ports misaki's lexicon logic (Apache-2.0).
   snippets/ort-web-*/: Brev ORT loader (MIT or Apache-2.0 as Brev's); _telemetry.js is a no-op.

Not included: espeak-ng (GPL-3.0). No GPL code ships with the neural voice.
