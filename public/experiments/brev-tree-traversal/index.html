The question

Can a small encoder replace a large model if you hand it the intents as a tree of short questions?

What this page is

A replay. Every probability below was recorded from a real laya run on the 3,080 Banking77 test messages. There is no live model here and no box to type your own message.

How to read it

At each node laya scores every option; the walk follows the highest one. A wrong turn near the top cannot be undone further down.

Walk the tree

Sample, not benchmark

These are 60 of the 3,080 test messages, chosen with seed 42: 10 correct, 10 wrong at the root, 10 wrong lower down, 10 with the lowest margins, and the 20 shortest. Errors are over-represented on purpose, so do not read accuracy off this list. Message text is public Banking77.

    The verdict in the ledger

    laya as a substitute for the 27B is refuted: the walk lands 30.8 points below the 27B short prompt (95% CI 28.9 to 32.8). Its strength is speed and shortlisting, not this walk. Holding the lowest-margin 15% of walks for the 27B lifts the tree from 47.0% to 55.2% in the ledger.

    Replay check. Re-running the greedy walk from the recorded probabilities gives 1,447 of 3,080 correct, 46.98%, which matches the ledger's 47.0% for this tree.

    Three ways the walk fails

    It obeys the text, even when wrong

    8.9%

    Give each option a sibling's criterion, keep the names, and accuracy falls from 47.0% to 8.9% (38.1 points, 95% CI 36.3 to 40.0). laya follows the criteria text over the option name.

    Names alone are not enough

    36.8%

    Names only: 36.8%. Opaque ids with the criteria: 40.6%. Both: 47.0%. Dropping the criteria costs 10.2 points (95% CI 8.5 to 12.0). The criteria carry most of the knowledge.

    Wrong turns compound

    0.72 × 0.72 × 0.79

    Per-level accuracy in the ledger: 71.9%, 71.9% and 78.8% (level 3 over 2,200 messages). Multiplied, about 0.41. A wrong group at the root cannot be recovered below it, and raw beam search made it worse (40.5% against 47.0%).

      Caveats

      Read this first

      Scores are precomputed from one real laya run and replayed here. Nothing on this page calls a model, and there is no way to enter your own message.
      • One dataset (Banking77), one run, one authored tree. The result may not carry to other intent sets.
      • The 60 samples are stratified toward errors and short texts. They illustrate behaviour; they do not estimate accuracy.
      • Arms differ in model, prompt and hardware, so the comparison table is descriptive. Latencies are not like-for-like: laya ran locally, the 27B figure includes network and a shared queue.
      • The flat laya tournament used every fifth test message (n=616); its figure has a wider interval than the 3,080-message rows.
      • Bars show laya's option probabilities rounded to three decimals. Gold intents are the Banking77 labels. The tree was authored by a large language model, not tuned on these messages.
      • The per-level and compounding figures are the ledger's own, quoted as written; they were not independently re-derived on this page.
