Hosted office suites keep your files where the provider can read them. I wrote up an idea that flips the default: mail and documents stay on your own devices, and the cloud only stores encrypted copies. Collaboration still has to work, so the write-up goes through offline edits, merging, recovery and what leaks anyway.

<!-- owner:question Which part do you think is hardest? The description promises "where it gets hard", but the body does not pick one. -->

Nothing here is built or measured yet; it's just a design sketch. The [idea page](/ideas/decentralized-office/) has the diagrams along with a quiz and a question box.
