Hosted office suites keep your work where the provider can read it.
This is an idea for keeping mail and documents on your own devices, with the cloud holding only encrypted copies.
Collaboration still has to work, so the explainer covers offline edits, merging, recovery and what leaks anyway.
It is an idea, not a product. Nothing on it is built or measured.
The [idea page](/ideas/decentralized-office/) has the diagrams, a quiz and a question box.
