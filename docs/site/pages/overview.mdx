## Verdict

**A live personal site with two open fixes and no written vision yet.** The site is built and deployed on every merge to `master`. This document tracks its open work. The one thing needed from the owner is the vision: see [Q-1](#Q-1).

### The verdict in five lines

1. The site is live: posts, research reports, playground demos and ideas ([Plan](#plan)).
2. Two fixes are open: broken links on one report, and page CSS leaking onto every page ([Plan](#plan)).
3. The vision is the owner's to write, so it waits on [Q-1](#Q-1).
4. Agents write the code; the owner approves posts in their own voice.
5. Every change passes CI, including a leak scan, before it merges.

### Status and caveats

<Good title="Built">
The site, its CI checks (build, browser checks, leak scan, secret scan) and deployment to GitHub Pages.
</Good>

<Warn title="Not written yet">
The vision, readers and success measures. Requirements, architecture and the other design pages are marked not applicable until the vision exists.
</Warn>
