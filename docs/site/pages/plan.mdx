## Stages

### Stages

<Cards>
| Stage | Build | Measure | Stop if |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Open fixes | W-1 and W-2 | The weekly link check is clean; no Fuwari page carries fragment CSS | A fix breaks a CI check that passed before |
| 2. Vision | The owner answers Q-1; the design pages follow from it | The overview states the vision in the owner's words | The owner decides the doc is not worth keeping |
</Cards>

### Dates

The stages have no dates. Stage 1 runs as fixes land; stage 2 waits on the owner.

## Work packages

### Packages

The current stages, broken into stories: each one reviewable change, with `phase:` naming its stage. A story too big for one change is split into tasks, W-n.1 and W-n.2, and finishes when they do. Later stages are broken down when the data from earlier ones exists. The document is the epic. Live state is on [Status](#status). Add a story with `sdlc new work "title"`.

Any story or task may name its Jira issue with `jira: PAY-12`, and `jira_epic: "PAY-1"` in `sdlc.yaml` names the document's epic. Readers turn the links on from the Ledger tab with the project's URL; `jira:` in `sdlc.yaml` turns them on for everyone, at the cost of putting the Jira site's address in the page.

## How the work is run

### Built by agents, decided by people

- Agents write the code and the tests.
- A person approves each stage gate and every change to the trusted base.
- Agents decide every call they can, after a cross-model brainstorm recorded as a decision. They ask the owner only for what needs the owner's authority (scope, preferences, money, accounts, legal, anything irreversible outside the repo), with options, recommended first.
- Every work item names the requirements it covers.
- An item is done when its "done when" test passes and the evidence is recorded. A claim with no evidence is not done.
- Reviews are cross-model: the model that wrote a change does not review it.
- A change that adds a trusted component or a number to this document updates the trusted-base table or a claim record in the same change.

### One coordinator, self-driven builders

Builders run the work; the coordinator only decides what the ladder sends it.

- **Take**: a builder that finishes an item takes the next in the same turn. Only a builder with nothing ready is woken, once per item handed to it.
- **Split**: a builder splits an item it holds into at most four children, two levels deep, inside the parent's `where`, at most 1.5 times its weight. Children are W-n.1, W-n.2. The parent stays as a milestone.
- **Find**: work found outside an item is filed as proposed, and is scheduled once another builder acks it.
- **Scope**: `where` is the write scope. Free paths are granted by the artifact server's task API. A held path gets one offer to its holder by hook line; with no answer in ten minutes, the later item parks behind it.
- **Prove**: a change check fails on the base and passes on the candidate; keep checks pass on both. A builder who is not the author reviews M, L and contract items.

| Level | Who | Budget | Goes up when |
|---|---|---|---|
| L0 | The builder | One attempt plus one fix after a bounce | The second bounce, or a path another claim holds |
| L1 | One peer | One offer, one counter, ten minutes, no wake | A cycle, the critical path, or the same dispute twice |
| L2 | Coordinator | Batched, at most three wakes an hour | A decision beyond its delegated authority |
| L3 | Owner | Only the items that list the question wait | Nowhere: the owner decides |

An escalation carries the item, the evidence, what was tried, two options and a recommendation. A bare "stuck" is refused.

### Definition of done for a stage

1. Every package's test passes.
2. The stage's measurements are recorded as claims.
3. No stop condition is met, or one is met and the owner decides.
4. This document is updated: decisions, owner questions, claims.

### The ids

R requirement, D decision, K risk, W story, Q owner question, C claim. A task under story W-n is W-n.k, made by splitting W-n; work found outside an item is a new story. Ids are never reused or renumbered. Each has an anchor, so a link to `#` plus the id goes straight to it.
